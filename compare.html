What CaptionVid does not do

CaptionVid is a captions app, not a full video editor: it has no music, stickers, filters or transitions, and no AI-generated video. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where the others do more

Captions Lite offers AI dubbing, eye-contact correction, AI zooms and sounds, and captions in 100+ languages. CapCut is a full video and photo editor with templates, effects and cloud storage. Both install and update from Google Play.
