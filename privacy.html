Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the CaptionVid app for Android (package uk.jj.captionvid) and this website. CaptionVid is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Your videos, their sound and their captions stay on your phone. Speech recognition runs on the phone.
  • There are no accounts, no servers of our own, no advertising and no analytics.
  • CaptionVid goes online only to download the speech model and translation languages, and when you tap Check for updates.

What stays on your phone

Each project (the words, their timings, your edits and a small still of the video) is kept in CaptionVid's private storage. Settings, saved looks and brand kits are kept there too. Exported videos are saved in Movies/CaptionVid and subtitle files in Download/CaptionVid, where they are yours. Android's cloud backup is switched off for CaptionVid.

When CaptionVid goes online

WhatWhereWhen
Speech modelHugging Face (huggingface.co), where the open whisper.cpp models are publishedOnce, when you choose a model
Translation languagesGoogle, through Google Play services' on-phone translation (ML Kit)Once per language, the first time you translate into it

None of these requests carries your videos, sound or captions. As with any download, those sites see your IP address. Google's on-phone translation may send Google basic usage and performance information about the translation feature, under Google's ML Kit terms.

Keeping and deleting your data

Delete a project from its menu in Projects. Uninstalling CaptionVid, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes every project, setting and downloaded model. Neither touches your original videos or the exports in Movies/CaptionVid.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the app list. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
